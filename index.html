Global Ship Lease Enters Newbuilding Market with $917M Order

In a landmark move, Global Ship Lease (NYSE: GSL) placed its first-ever newbuilding order: 10 mid-size, ultra-high-reefer, wide-beam containerships for approximately $917 million. Deliveries are scheduled from Q4 2028 to Q1 2030. All 10 vessels are pre-chartered for an average of 6.7 years, generating an estimated $665 million in adjusted EBITDA. The company currently holds $2.1 billion in contracted forward charter coverage.
